# Vidnov: Video-First Social Commerce

Vidnov is a modern marketplace designed for the next generation of entrepreneurs, creators, and shoppers. It transforms the shopping experience into an interactive, video-driven ecosystem.

## 🚀 How It Works

Vidnov operates as a three-sided marketplace connecting **Sellers**, **Resellers**, and **Buyers** through a unified digital platform.

### 🎥 For Sellers (The Creators)
*   **Authentic Content:** Upload short-form videos to showcase products in a high-conversion, TikTok-style feed.
*   **Live Studio:** Broadcast real-time shopping events with integrated chat, pinned products, and "Flash Deal" triggers.
*   **AI Toolkit:** Use built-in AI (Genkit) to generate descriptions, social media captions, and animated marketing videos.
*   **Growth:** Set commission rules to enable a network of resellers to market your products globally.

### 💸 For Resellers (The Amplifiers)
*   **Zero Inventory:** Earn money by promoting products you love without ever holding stock.
*   **Unique Tracking:** Generate personalized links for any "resellable" product and share them on WhatsApp, TikTok, or Instagram.
*   **Passive Income:** Automated commission calculations added directly to your digital wallet upon successful sales.
*   **Rewards:** Earn AI Points by referring new users and climb the tiers to unlock exclusive prizes.

### 🛒 For Buyers (The Shoppers)
*   **Watch & Shop:** Discover products through an immersive video feed where "what you see is what you get."
*   **Live Engagement:** Interact with sellers during live broadcasts, ask questions, and secure real-time discounts.
*   **Secure Payments:** Local and international payment support via Paystack (M-Pesa) and PayPal.
*   **Trust:** Verified seller profiles and integrated chat ensure a safe and transparent shopping journey.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

*   **Frontend:** Next.js 15 (App Router), React, Tailwind CSS, Lucide Icons.
*   **UI Components:** Shadcn/UI (Radix UI).
*   **Backend:** Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Functions).
*   **AI Intelligence:** Firebase Genkit with Google Gemini models.
*   **Live Streaming:** LiveKit for low-latency real-time signals (self-hosted on our own VPS — no per-minute fees).

## 💼 Admin & Oversight
The platform includes a robust Admin Dashboard for monitoring system health, moderating content, managing payouts, and overseeing the AI recommendation engines.
